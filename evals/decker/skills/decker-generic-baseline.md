# Reviewing the change

Give the assembled change a thorough, skeptical review. Some guidance:

- Take your time and look at the whole change, not just the obvious parts.
- Don't assume the tests cover everything — passing tests can hide real
  problems.
- When something looks suspicious, dig into it rather than moving on.
- Prefer concrete evidence over hunches: if you believe something is wrong,
  confirm it before reporting it.
- Be thorough but fair — report the problems that are actually there, and
  don't invent problems that aren't.
